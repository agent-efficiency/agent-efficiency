# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a practical, respectful, and safe project.

Contributors must:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give specific, evidence-based feedback;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and sexualized conduct;
- stop unwanted contact;
- follow maintainer decisions about project scope and safety.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Do not post private evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or ban a participant. They
will consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project
in another public space.
